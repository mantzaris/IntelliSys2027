The root diagnostic fixes incoming summaries separately within each transport:
selective repetition of each candidate objective, uniform repetition of all
objectives, decisive-constraint repetition, and neutral/position controls. It has
no paraphrase arm. Lossless transport carries original worker propositions and is
a separate idealized preservation intervention. The strict pre-outcome analysis
amendment separates insufficient, contradictory, ill-typed and sufficient visible
information. It never repairs a summary using the evaluator's values. Possible
answers under some consistent completion and an answer justified under every
completion are different fields. Domain constraints are those of the public
report interface (integer nonnegative objectives and mandatory conjunction), not
unseen job data. A relaxed answer set for disputed values is explicitly separate.
Six-base unstratified bootstrap intervals are descriptive; the original
family/change-stratified intervals remain unavailable.

The prior conditional mechanism confirmation does not compare verified
paraphrases or selective versus balanced **objective** repetition. A separate
bounded confirmation therefore uses 24 fresh scheduling pairs, four in each
numeric/identity/dependency × consequential/harmless stratum, both worlds, one
stochastic repetition, ordinary hierarchical synthesis, and both model families.
Its five arms are single, selective exact, selective paraphrase, balanced exact,
and balanced paraphrase. Candidate rank cycles across the three candidate IDs;
the selected candidate stays fixed across worlds. Twelve of 48 target contexts
favor the correct candidate and 16 concern an infeasible candidate. This avoids
selecting only favorable reminders. All mandatory findings remain present.

`configs/frozen/objective-confirmation-v1.json` and the frozen analysis manifest
fix identities, seeds, arms, contrasts, missing-output handling and resource cap.
Normalized structural comparison excludes exact isomorphic pairs and candidates
with at most two changed leaf fields, removing names, absolute time and candidate
ordering. The resulting population is a structurally filtered scheduling sample;
it shares generator motifs and is not an estimate over the original prefix's case
mixture. No model response enters this selection.

The three primary model-specific accuracy contrasts are selective exact minus
single, selective paraphrase minus single, and balanced minus selective averaged
over exact/paraphrase. Direct exact-minus-paraphrase and paired-world responses
are secondary. Resampling retains whole base pairs, stratified by family and
change status. No confirmatory p-values, power claims, pooling of incomplete
models, or outcome-dependent sample extensions are planned. The original study
supplies flat/hierarchy contrasts; the new small design does not duplicate them.
Balanced objectives add nine reports versus three for selective emphasis, so the
contrast combines evidence balance with length/dose. Existing length controls
provide diagnostic context, not automatic removal of this confound.
