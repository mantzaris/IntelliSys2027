# Manuscript reference verification

Checked 17 October 2026. The paper cites 21 distinct entries: 19 research papers and two official model cards. Full ordered metadata, exact versions, and claim support are in [reference-verification-v6.json](records/reference-verification-v6.json).

Primary published records take precedence over preprints. The self-consistency camera-ready PDF explicitly identifies ICLR 2023. OpenReview presented a browser challenge, so its metadata was cross-checked against that primary author deposit. D3MAS remains documented as withdrawn in the historical related-work record and is not cited in this draft.
