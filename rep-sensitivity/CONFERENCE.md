# IntelliSys 2027 verification

Checked 2026-10-16 UTC against the [official call](https://saiconference.com/IntelliSys/CallforPapers).
Rounds close 1 November and 1 December 2026, AoE. Normal limits are 18 main-text
pages plus up to 7 pages of references/appendices, and a 150–250-word abstract.
Initial PDF submission is anonymous and uses the supplied template. The downloaded
[LaTeX archive](https://saiconference.com/Downloads/SAI_Paper_Format_Latex_Conference.zip)
uses `svproc`; preserve its dimensions. Do not include identities or acknowledgments.

The AI policy requires disclosure and human responsibility, excludes AI authorship,
and reserves development of scientific ideas, arguments, results and conclusions
to human authors. Automated research execution here goes beyond language editing.
Human review and possible clarification with the organizers are therefore needed
before claiming eligibility. The assistant will not contact organizers or submit.

Proposed truthful disclosure for the author's review:

> The author supplied the research direction and experimental requirements. An
> AI coding assistant helped implement and test the benchmark, operate local
> open-weight model experiments, retrieve literature, generate analyses, and draft
> text. Its methodological decisions and interpretations require author verification.
> This working draft has not yet undergone the author's scientific review.

Replace the last sentence only after actual review, and describe the human author's
final scientific contributions accurately. Do not claim AI use was limited to
language polishing, and do not name AI as an author.
