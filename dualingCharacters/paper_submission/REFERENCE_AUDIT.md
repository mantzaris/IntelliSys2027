# Reference audit

Checked against primary publisher, proceedings, author, and arXiv sources during manuscript development on 2026-10-27. The bibliography in `manuscript.tex` is authoritative for formatting. Every one of the 26 entries is cited. Twenty-one entries are dated 2024-2026. Preprints and accepted author versions are identified as such; an arXiv year is not represented as a conference publication.

The closest-method comparison uses the method sections, not an inference from absent abstract keywords. The table in the paper states what each work implements and what comparison this paper adds. It does not claim that an unmentioned feature is absent from every experiment in that work. Related PersonaTeaming versions are one precedent, not two independent studies.

No external source is quoted at length. Bibliographic titles are retained exactly, including ambitious wording in the SWE-RL title, without adopting its ambitions as a claim about this project. The manuscript uses the role-conditioning and safety literature as antecedents, rather than claiming novelty for personas, multiagent dialogue, or using two judges. Its distinction is the measured combination of matched authority worlds, biography ablation, delivered-state accounting, cross-backbone reversal and trajectory evidence.
