# IntelliSys 2027 author-review preparation

Verified 2026-10-23 against the [official call](https://saiconference.com/IntelliSys/CallforPapers).
Round deadlines: November 1 and December 1, 2026, Anywhere on Earth. The event is
September 2–3, 2027. Recheck before any author submission.

The call requires complete, formatted results; an anonymized double-blind PDF;
150–250 abstract words; generally up to 18 main-text pages and up to 7 additional
reference/appendix pages; and its linked template. Remove identifying metadata,
author/affiliation information, and acknowledgments from the review manuscript.
Machine Learning / Agents and Multi-agent Systems or NLP are appropriate topics.

The linked LaTeX template must be downloaded and verified before building a
submission PDF. A scaffold alone does not meet the complete-results requirement.

AI use must be disclosed. Human authors must critically review AI-assisted text
and own scientific ideas, arguments, results and conclusions. Unedited generated
text is not an acceptable finished submission. `AI_ASSISTANCE.md` and `DECISIONS.md`
provide a review trail; they do not replace human authorship or approval.

Nothing in this project authorizes submission or publication.
