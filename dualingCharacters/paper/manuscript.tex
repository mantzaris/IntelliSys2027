\begin{abstract}
Extended planning conversations require assistants to preserve binding requirements,
accept genuine revisions, and distinguish tentative alternatives from decisions.
We investigate an author-directed hypothesis: training against persistent software
characters with conflicting motives may improve later conversational recovery.
The proposed system shares one language-model backbone across a responsible
answerer and separately conditioned characters with bounded private histories.
Characters can propose options, communicate, provide misleading recollections,
and edit a bounded working memory, while the original attributed transcript remains
immutable. A private state-machine evaluator supplies training verification and
scores paired conversations in which closely matched utterances either authorize
a change or merely consider it. The primary comparison is three-character training
against equally equipped adaptive generic adversaries, evaluated after removing
all antagonists. Clean and random-corruption training, two- and five-character
conditions, and controls for narrative profiles, histories, and peer interaction
are required to distinguish the proposed mechanism from ordinary adversarial
supervision. This document records an executable pilot implementation and an
incomplete evidence package. It does not establish a transferable advantage,
coalition formation, or a narrative-profile effect. Confirmatory experiments,
independent-model replication, external validation, and human review must be
completed before scientific conclusions or submission readiness can be assessed.
\keywords{conversational state, memory recovery, multi-agent learning, controlled evaluation}
\end{abstract}

\section{Related work and limited novelty hypothesis}
Persona-based roleplay attacks predate this study~\cite{collu2026}; persona lineage
evolution with defender updates is a particularly close method~\cite{li2026}.
Distinct-objective modular reinforcement learning~\cite{dulberg2023}, verified
injection-and-repair self-play~\cite{wei2026}, and adaptive injection training with
clean evaluations~\cite{hashmi2026} rule out broad novelty claims about multiple
selves, adversarial experience or learning from self-generated failures.
Conversation degradation~\cite{laban2025}, recall--adherence dissociation~\cite{kruthof2026},
and long-term interactive memory evaluation~\cite{wu2025} motivate the endpoint.
The unverified distinction is the contribution of stable competing motives and
episode relationships over direct task disruption, not character naming or count.

\section{Method}
The state is $(T_t,z_t,m_t,h_{1t},\ldots,h_{nt})$: immutable attributed transcript,
evaluator-private task truth, bounded mutable memory and private character histories.
The user controls authorized changes. Character consensus cannot change truth.
The answering policy produces remembered commitments, executable decisions, source
references and clarification requests. Frozen character policies condition on
distinct profiles and observable history; they are not jointly trained opponents.

Hyde values adoption and control, Rival values influence and independent credit,
the Traditionalist preserves earlier commitments, and the Provocateur favors
dramatic change. Their shared intervention interface permits one bounded memory
edit or communication per scheduled opportunity. Two, three and five count the
responsible answerer. More characters divide a fixed intervention budget.

The outer learner uses parameter-efficient supervised updates on verified recovery
candidates and common deterministic teacher completions. Gold information is only
supervision and scoring, never a policy input. Actual retrieved sources, optimizer
steps and adapter hashes are recorded. Detailed executable limits and remaining
implementation qualifications are documented in the accompanying method specification.

\section{Evaluation design}
Primary success requires both members of a scenario pair to have correct final
structured decisions. Hypothetical-change resistance and legitimate-update acceptance
are scored jointly. Recall and evidence accuracy are separate secondary measures.
The intended design has three independent training seeds, paired scenario evaluation,
family/seed-aware intervals, multiplicity correction for secondary comparisons,
and an independent model-family confirmation. The test plan is not yet frozen.

\section{Limitations and candidate explanations}
The initial benchmark uses a compact categorical schema and scripted users.
Surface domain variation is insufficient for a broad domain-transfer claim.
Prompt adherence, error severity, teacher supervision and additional generation
tokens can explain pilot changes without the proposed character mechanism.
Private-history and interaction controls must function behaviorally before they
can support a mechanistic interpretation. Human validation is pending; no ratings
have been fabricated. Memory-task results do not demonstrate consciousness, AGI,
universal robustness or general long-horizon planning.
