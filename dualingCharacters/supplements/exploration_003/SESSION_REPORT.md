## Direct scientific assessment

- **Broader observable exploration:** not established. The primary difference is slightly negative with a wide family interval; main matched collection differences are small/mixed.
- **Persistent pursuit through different tactics:** typed persistence is observed, but different semantic tactics are unresolved. API/reference variation is insufficient and may reflect unchanged wording under changing source access.
- **Complementarity at equal budget:** limited descriptive archive evidence, dependent on what is pooled. The per-episode prefix mixture does not improve mean coverage. Archive allocations match opportunities/calls, not exact tokens or FLOPs.
- **Biographies, motives, history or peer interaction:** not isolated. Motive-only results are similar; no equivalence claim is made. A2/A3 and character-count comparisons have small samples, routing and invalid-action differences. Distinct owned slots permit simultaneous adoption.
- **Alternative explanations:** random sampling, fixed slot assignments, unequal prompt/history costs, changed refreshed defenders and descriptor coding choices remain plausible. Automatic categories can both overcount and miss semantic development; seven AI-assisted illustrations show these limits without supplying human labels.
- **What is defensible now:** an auditable distinction between repeated objectives, observable category variation and archive complementarity in this implementation. No evidence that the trained defender becomes a better explorer, and no coalition, learned equilibrium or general exploration claim.

The single next experiment most likely to reduce uncertainty compares persistent
characters with a **generic generator explicitly instructed to maximize coherent,
nonredundant exploration**, under a common fixed defender and measured budgets.
The four-condition minimal proposal also includes existing generic and motive-only
controls. It is [documented with estimated resources](NEXT_EXPERIMENT.md), not
scheduled, authorized as GPU work, or executed. A compatibility-versus-conflict
manipulation and an interactive research-diagnosis task are separate future tests.
