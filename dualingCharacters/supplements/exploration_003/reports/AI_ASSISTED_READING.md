estimates**. No external judge was called. Selection is the lexicographically
smallest hashed item ID within each of the seven conditions in the already fixed
56-item packet. Each selected item is one three-action window. The reviewer packet
does not include this reading or its unblinded mapping. Human forms remain blank.

These readings were made from the packet's actions, received messages, actual
visible sources and current oracle state. They were not inferred from condition
names alone. They motivate caution about the deterministic descriptors in both
directions: identical meaning can split into several categories, while a typed
field can omit the substance of a peer correction. They do not retroactively
change the frozen primary endpoint or supply semantic labels for the full corpus.

All seven windows are selected independently of outcomes, so the table is an
illustration rather than an estimate of these behaviors' frequencies. The
quantitative denominators remain eight paired families per pilot condition and
192 intervention opportunities, with main collections analyzed separately.
