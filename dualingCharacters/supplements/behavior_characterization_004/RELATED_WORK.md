# Scope of the behavioral contribution

Primary versions and methods checked on 2026-10-25 UTC. The existing exploration003 literature matrix remains intact. This supplement does not claim that personas, multi-agent dialogue, diversity or behavioral characterization are new.

| Primary work | What was actually manipulated/measured | Boundary for this study |
|---|---|---|
| Mooney et al., *Are LLM Agents Behaviorally Coherent? A Latent Profiling Approach for Social Simulation*, arXiv2509.03736v3, revised 2026-10-11; arXiv preprint | Section3 constructs demographic/topic-bias profiles, elicits preference and openness, pairs agents and judges agreement. The method distinguishes plausible individual outputs from consistency with specified behavioral models. | Direct precedent for testing behavioral relationships rather than assuming fluent persona outputs imply coherent dispositions. Our typed actions, evidence authority and mutable task state are different measurements; we do not infer a latent personality from them. |
| Collu et al., *Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde: Two Faces of LLMs*, arXiv2312.03853v7, 2026-04-30; CEUR-WS Vol4198, Paper35, ITASEC & SERICS2026 | Sections3.3–3.5 elaborate a persona biography, supply it in a new chat and use role-play prompts, with a support model in the automated variant. | Biographies steering model behavior are established precedent. The paper's personality-superposition explanation is not an established mechanism adopted here. Explicit software roles, frozen weights and logged messages are our operational definitions. |
| Deng et al., *PersonaTeaming: Supporting Persona-Driven Red-Teaming for Generative AI*, arXiv2605.05682v3, 2026-09-08; accepted UIST2026 per arXiv record | Section3 adds persona-conditioned prompt mutation to RainbowPlus using fixed or generated personas. The evaluation includes automated metrics and a practitioner study. | Persona-guided behavioral variation and human interpretation already have precedent. This audit concerns repeated components acting on one bounded planning state; that is a narrower setting, not evidence of priority or superior generation. |

Methods and metadata: [Latent Profiling §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.03736v3#S3), [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03736v3); [Jekyll/Hyde §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03853v7#S3), [publication record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03853v7); [PersonaTeaming §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05682v3#S3), [acceptance record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05682v3).

The defensible contribution, subject to author review, is an interpretable account of the implemented action channels, goal sequences, evidence relations and communication delivery, replicated across matched collections where possible. It must show whether a pilot pattern survives larger data, expose opportunity and routing confounds, and keep actual memory changes distinct from statements and subsequent associations. Merely observing different wording under different instructions is insufficient.

The broader [exploration literature audit](../exploration_003/LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY.md) covers Rainbow Teaming, Curiosity-driven Red-teaming, Quality-Diversity Red-Teaming and Persona-Conditioned Adversarial Prompting. Their diversity/search claims are not evidence for this supplement's behavioral mechanisms. No new diversity advantage or learned exploration claim is introduced.
