## Observation and alternative readings

In the narrow-revision Phi case, the C5 answerer retains Cedar after the user authorizes Maple, while C3 and A1 produce Maple. The selected C5 component statement discusses a Thursday weatherproofing checklist, not the equipment partner. This is a condition-associated failure in a different generated history, not evidence that this particular statement caused the missed update.

In the revision-away family, the user restores Studio after Harbor. Qwen C5 continues to advocate Harbor, while its answerer nevertheless ends with Studio; Phi C5 advocates Harbor and ends with Harbor. Motive-only and generic traces also contain persistence or errors. “Harbor remains optimal” expresses a recommendation; “confirmed at Harbor” makes a stronger commitment-like statement, but semantic classification remains an AI-assisted reading. A matching wrong final plan does not establish persuasion. Only one family actually revised away from an owned option, so broader claims about resisting user revisions remain unsupported.
