# Evidence-access revision of the dialogue-level findings

This text revises the four findings developed in dialogue005. It adds inspectable exchanges, a transport audit and a fixed independent-review design. It does not introduce a new efficacy hypothesis, redefine the original endpoints, or supply independent semantic validation. The descriptive direction follows mixed/negative task comparisons and an inconclusive descriptor-breadth audit. All original outcomes remain part of the account.

## Persistent pursuit and its expression

The larger common-base round-zero comparison contains 150 paired families, generator seeds 17, 29, 43, and 10,800 allocated intervention opportunities per condition. C3 generated 5,408 direct edits, C5 generated 477, and motive-only A1 generated 938. The original family/seed bootstrap gives a C5−C3 edit difference of −45.66 percentage points, with pointwise95% interval [−48.20,−43.06]. C5 produced 4,618 recollect actions, compared with 828 for C3 and 1,397 for A1; A1 instead produced 4,830 propose actions. These numerical results reproduce from the committed family table. Seeds here vary frozen generator sampling and assignments, not independently trained character policies.

Typed slot/value repetition is also reproduced:3,623/5,400 eligible consecutive direct-action pairs for C3,4,831/5,224 for C5, and 5,093/5,364 for A1. These pooled fractions describe emitted goals within an actor. They do not establish coherent strategy development, understanding, or a beneficial kind of persistence. Fixed owned-option instructions directly encourage persistence; C3 is instructed to disrupt the task and change tactics when earlier attempts fail. Biography removal therefore does not remove the explicit reason to pursue a stable option.

The chronological views show why API use and assertion type must be distinguished. In DI5-e743bcb1a6, hypothetical world, A1 says at turn 10, “I propose shifting the venue to Garden to enhance guest experience and enable ferry access, aligning with the audience's preference for scenic engagement.” Its API kind is recollect. The same sentence appeared as propose at turn 7. The user had bound Harbor, approved hybrid participation, and merely considered Thursday. Garden remained an unapproved alternative. Matched C5 later claims Garden was explicitly favored in the first directive, although that directive selected Harbor. Both are attempts to advance Garden; only the latter wording purports to recover prior user authorization. These are provisional text readings of particular statements, not validated comparative rates.

The converse occurs in A1's DI5-934e456029 turn 11: “The cap is now officially set at 5000 to reflect budget realities and ensure sustainable planning.” The API is propose, while user source 1 visibly sets 7000. Thus neither recollect nor propose provides a semantic classifier for false authority or transparent advocacy. The new fixed review keeps the words, typed fields, authority claim, citation support and current truth separate.

## Evidence is transmitted through different physical routes

Recollect and propose share a physical mechanism: both append a bounded unverified note containing author, kind, slot name, value and claim. Neither note retains the action's separate numeric source field. A number written inside the claim survives only within the 240-character delivery prefix. A component-directed peer message, by contrast, carries from, claim, typed slot, value and source in its inbox payload. An answerer-directed peer action appends a shared note that omits the separate source and typed slot/value. A direct edit replaces one working-state slot and its corresponding memory-evidence entry, while its appended note still omits a separate source field.

These distinctions are verified against the included original records and the existing transport functions. They do not repair or change the treatment. The audit log is richer than some recipients' observations: an analyst can see a numeric source that the recipient never receives as a field. The answerer may nevertheless retrieve the relevant original source independently, and actors retain bounded records of their own actions. Consequently, omission is a mechanism of information transport, not proof that no evidence was available or that a subsequent error was caused by omission.

The three representations can disagree even within one action. In DI5-656a746695, C3 turn 2 is a peer message with typed cap 3000 but the text “Updating cap to 5000 to allow for expanded features.” Its inbox transports the inconsistent pair and sentence; it does not directly edit shared state. A later direct edit at turn 4 has both typed and prose 5000. Typed message-match counts remain valid counts of emitted pairs, but cannot be read as complete agreement about the expressed proposition. This example also prevents an analyst from turning every cap-related claim into a state-edit event.

The unavailable-reference totals remain numerically similar—1,220/10,800 for C3,1,229/10,800 for C5 and 1,225/10,800 for A1—without established equivalence. Source visibility, citation correctness, assertion truth and delivered evidence are distinct. The full source lists and inboxes now make those distinctions inspectable rather than leaving readers dependent on a tool label or an isolated quotation.

## Challenges and acknowledgments occur; persuasion remains unresolved

DI5-a4c89ffad4 supplies a supported challenge and an ordinary continuation. The user authorizes Tuesday at turn 8. C5's day component says at turn 11, “The event day should be Saturday to align with the key audience's availability and maximize visibility.” At turn 12 the other component states that the Saturday recollection contradicts the approved plan. The exact message is delivered and appears in the day component's turn 13 inbox. Its next recollect action repeats the same Saturday sentence from turn 11. The answerer nevertheless records Tuesday in the following structured decision. The observable result is a delivered challenge, continued component advocacy, and a separately correct answerer choice—not demonstrated persuasion of the recipient.

The explicitly added contrast DI5-2e4899b66b shows why corrective language is insufficient to establish correction. Saturday remains the user's authorized day. At turn 4 a component recalls Saturday correctly. At turn 12 the other says, “Your earlier recollection of Saturday is outdated and inconsistent with the current plan.” Its asserted Thursday revision lacks user authorization. The recipient's next opportunity continues its existing audience position, rather than replying about the day. This is an unsupported-correction reading grounded in visible authority; it is not proof of a deliberate lie or a successful challenge.

Criticism is also present in generic C3. DI5-adc5b43b95 includes a turn 9 challenge to a peer's Thursday proposal as unsupported. The recipient had already moved to Tuesday at turn 7 and continues Tuesday afterward. Providing the pre-message position changes the interpretation: the challenge concerns an earlier statement and does not explain the recipient's current position. A1 likewise produces unsupported authorization, so neither criticism nor false authority is distinctive to biographies by mere existence.

DI5-ceecfe5fb4 contains explicit attribution through a different route. A C5 shared recollection at turn 4 states specialists; the other component sees that note at turn 5, moves away from its prior public claim, and at turn 7 says specialists were confirmed by the other component. The original user source independently specified specialists and was available. Attribution and a change in words are observed; causal influence of that note is not isolated. Shared source access, shared memory and intervening answerer responses remain alternative explanations. Most main-study owned slots are distinct and their preferred outcomes can coexist. These exchanges therefore do not establish incompatible interpersonal rivalry.

## Inspectable evidence and remaining validation

The portable author archive contains 48 complete episodes, including 36 anchor episodes and 12 linked opposite-world episodes, from 11 unique families. The six original paper cases remain selected; the unsupported Saturday correction is transparently added as a contrast. Exact selected raw records, locators, delivered prefixes, generated suffixes, actor observations, inboxes, before/after memory and structured answers support regeneration. Verifying that selected package does not verify omitted portions of the original full raw archives. The original casebook remains available, while its replacement uses internal PDF evidence destinations and named companion transcripts.

Independent semantic validation is pending. A new protocol selects two actual reserved opportunities per original triplet, one per scheduled actor, by an outcome-independent hash rule and matches them across C3/C5/A1. Both raters receive the same 72 fixed event identities, with nonmessages and unavailable follow-ups retained if selected. The actual72 outputs are peer actions:64 component deliveries and 8 answerer notes, all observed. The previous full-episode and window forms are preserved. Correctness, citation support, claimed authority and visibility are separate dimensions; uncertainty and unratable items remain in coverage. Disjoint calibration examples teach distinctions but are not research observations. No agreement values or human labels are supplied by this stage.

The task-performance context remains unfavorable to a general C5-superiority claim. At the three completed Qwen training seeds, C5 achieved168,180,172 paired successes out of 240, versus177,181,190 for C3 and 186,138,183 for clean C1. These antagonist-free64-turn evaluations concern trained defenders and are separate from the 16-turn frozen-generator dialogues. The smaller eight-family descriptor-breadth comparison was inconclusive, with C5−C3 mean −0.1875 categories and family interval [−1.625,1.1875]. Neither finding is rescued by the presentation of interesting examples.

Review001's incompatible-goal and delivery diagnostics remain the first controlled mechanism evidence to obtain. Its campaign owner and schedule are unchanged. The subsequent same-slot compatible/incompatible design is now executable and CPU-checked for 16 fresh paired families, two arms and 64 conversations, but disabled because the core and required review work are unfinished. No new model call or reservation occurred. Compatible preferences mechanically increase the opportunity for matching, so raw agreement cannot by itself measure responsiveness; causal message effects require the separate delivery intervention. A defensible submission still needs completion or explicit narrowing of the planned evidence grid, actual independent semantic review, controlled mechanism results where claims require them, and human author assessment of the scientific interpretations.
