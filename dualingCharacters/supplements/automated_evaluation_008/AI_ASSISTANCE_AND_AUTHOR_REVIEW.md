# AI assistance and author review

This stage implements the author's October 26, 2026 decision to use automated semantic evaluation without recruited human annotation. That decision changes the current completion criteria; it does not retroactively turn historical forms into ratings, or turn model output into human validation.

Automated evaluation is an experimental method: two pinned, unadapted local model families received independently constructed checklist prompts under a frozen protocol. Their exact inputs, outputs, format repairs, calibration failures, missingness, and stability probes are retained. Controls have deterministic authored keys. No paid model API or external judge was used.

Separately, the coding assistant helped implement and execute the protocol, inspect artifacts, develop tests and analyses, verify sources, create figures, and draft the manuscript. The investigation followed an author-directed research brief and earlier observed results. Interpretive readings and proposed conclusions remain reviewable AI-assisted work; they are not independent human annotations.

Before submission, the human author must:

1. Review the evidence and approve the scientific scope, arguments, results, interpretations, and conclusions, including the failed measurement screens and negative task comparisons.
2. Check that the manuscript represents the author's scientific contribution and satisfies the current conference AI-use policy through critical review, editing, and accurate disclosure.
3. Supply and approve authorship, affiliations, acknowledgments, anonymous-submission handling, bibliography, and the final disclosure text.
4. Decide whether the bounded characterization is the intended paper, with the original campaign explicitly incomplete at its analysis cutoff. Later results require a separately dated integration, not silent replacement.

Recruitment of human raters is **not** a completion requirement. The manuscript states that no independent human semantic annotations were collected. No publication, submission, push, or external contact is authorized or performed by this stage.

Suggested disclosure, for author revision: “Automated local language-model judging was used as a reported experimental measurement, with synthetic calibration and sensitivity checks. AI assistance was also used in software development, analysis tooling, literature verification, visualization, and drafting. The authors critically reviewed and edited the scientific content and accept responsibility for the methods, results, interpretations, and conclusions.” The final sentence must only be adopted after that review actually occurs.
