# Reporting scope and chronology

The analysis cutoff is **2026-10-26 18:25:24.957430 UTC**. It includes 181 of 241 original stages and 37 completed main evaluation cells. Results after that time belong to a separately dated operational appendix, even when the original controller later regenerates its own broader report. No completed unfavorable cell at the cutoff is removed.

The new prospective automated-evaluation freeze occurred after development profiling and one allowed shared prompt revision. The overall behavioral direction remains exploratory: negative/mixed task comparisons, the inconclusive exploration audit, mechanical channel differences, and selected dialogue readings were already known. A prospective measurement freeze does not retrospectively preregister that direction.

The broad common-base generator population has 150 unique paired families and three generator seeds. Its 900 episodes per condition repeat those families. The new balanced semantic sample uses 15 hash-selected families, both worlds, all three generator seeds, and C3/C5/A1, with one direct and one reserved communication opportunity per cell. Its 540 opportunities are not 540 independent families. The older 72 fixed events have purposive family selection; they are a diagnostic sample rather than population prevalence evidence. The controlled compatibility population is 16 new paired families with 64 conversations, separately reported.

Automated model labels, deterministic facts, and controlled outcomes have separate artifacts. Source truth is time-indexed. A valid evidence span proves that text was present, not that its interpretation is correct. Model-extracted current authorization is compared to the ledger only after extraction; that comparison inherits extraction uncertainty. A recommendation is not automatically an authorization claim.

The compatibility experiment's primary descriptive endpoint is the next-recipient-action distribution over all reserved communication opportunities. Paired task success and plan correctness are secondary. Same-goal matching is easier by construction in compatible preferences. Prior-position transitions and actual visibility are therefore reported alongside matching. The separate review001 delivery intervention was not completed at this cutoff. No observational matching result is relabeled as causal message influence.

The final paper is a bounded author-review characterization, not a statement that the entire original learning, horizon, external-validation and recovery grid finished. Human semantic annotation was unavailable by author decision and was not collected. It is no longer a completion dependency. Human **author review** of scientific content, disclosure, scope, and submission preparation is still required.
