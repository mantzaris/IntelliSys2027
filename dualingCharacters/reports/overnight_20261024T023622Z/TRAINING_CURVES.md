# Training curves

Completed exposure-verified fits only, by model, condition, round and actual seed. Arithmetic means of 16 successive recorded optimizer-step losses are shown; zero means are plotted at 1e-8. Loss reflects selected supervised tokens and does not measure test generalization or permit direct curriculum-quality comparisons. Missing fits are pending, not zero-loss runs. Exact input and target counts are in the accompanying tables.
